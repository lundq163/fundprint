Welcome to Fundprint

Fundprint is a nonpartisan transparency tool. While it's on, it looks for the names of US federal candidates and members of Congress on the pages you browse, and gives them a subtle underline. Hover over one to see a summary of how that candidate's campaign is funded, sourced from official FEC filings.

One setup step: add your free FEC API key

Fundprint uses the official OpenFEC API, which requires a free API key. Your key is stored only on your own device (chrome.storage.local) and is never sent anywhere except directly to the FEC's servers.

  1. Get a free key at api.data.gov/signup.
  2. Paste it into the Fundprint options page.

Good to know

  • Fundprint presents facts only — no scores, labels, or ratings.
  • We never show individual donors' names, only their employer.
  • This beta runs on all sites you visit. See LIMITATIONS.md in the project for details, and use the popup to turn it off globally or per-site any time.
